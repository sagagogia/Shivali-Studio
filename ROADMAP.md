# Delivery roadmap

## Delivered local release

- Garment archive, MF03162 metadata, private asset intake and batch campaign creation.
- Persistent campaign briefs, deterministic Creative desk, editable captions/hooks/on-screen text/hashtags and music brief.
- Research evidence records with source type and timestamps; no invented trends.
- Technical decode plus manual garment QA, versioned approvals, audit history and estimated costs.
- Guarded fal image-edit bridge with mocked transport verification; live use untested and disabled.
- Real OpenMontage 27-second source-image previews, optional declared licensed audio, final human review and export.

## Milestone 2 — garment fidelity pilot

Upload original MF03162 photos to the app. Approve one concept and benchmark one image-edit request against fabric, embroidery, bead fringe, silhouette, necklace and dupatta. Reject or composite when fidelity fails. Validate paid-provider credentials, terms and actual invoice. Stop within the initial $5 experiment budget; no bulk generation before acceptance.

## Milestone 3 — cinematic motion and richer direction

Benchmark reference-conditioned image-to-video with current provider schemas and first/last frame constraints. Run temporal garment checks frame by frame and flag drift; never promise exact product preservation. Add agent-model creative direction, current source-backed research, differentiated concepts and campaign-specific shot execution. Each model/provider requires independent capability and commercial-term validation.

## Milestone 4 — production operations

Authenticated internal deployment, durable queue with cancellation and idempotency, normalized database, object storage, encrypted credential management, backups, monitoring, actual cost reconciliation, retention controls and team roles. Keep human gates and prove concurrency/restart safety.

## Milestone 5 — marketing distribution

Verified commercial music library with rights evidence, permitted Meta account insights, optional scheduled publishing through official APIs after account review, explicit per-publication approval and an export/publish audit. Introduce batch production only after garment-fidelity acceptance and budget enforcement have been exercised.

Indicative effort after this local release: garment pilot 1–2 weeks; motion/direction 1–2 weeks; secure operations and publishing 2–4 weeks, depending on provider access, source quality and approvals. These are planning estimates, not delivery commitments.
